# 3D_Jobs

Research for finding 3D modeling work at design studios, game studios and similar companies in the US.

## Files

- **US_3D_Design_Studios.xlsx** — the main spreadsheet, with three tabs:
  - **Design Studios** — 115 US studios that do 3D work (design consultancies, brand studios, creative tech, motion, CG/VFX, Seattle). Includes whether each had open 3D roles when checked (Oct 13–14, 2026).
  - **3D Job Sites** — 52 job boards, newsletters, freelance sites, staffing agencies and careers pages for 3D roles. The Source column shows which came from my research and which came from two outside lists.
  - **References & Removed** — reference studios (Teague, frog, IDEO), studios removed because they closed, and a key for the "Open 3D Roles?" column.
- **design_studios.csv** and **3d_job_sites.csv** — plain-text copies of the first two tabs, so they can be read right here on GitHub.

The spreadsheet is the main copy. The CSV files don't keep colors or clickable links.
